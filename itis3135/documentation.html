1. Getting Started

Our website is split into a few main pages:

  • Product Landing: an overview of the dealership and our featured cars.
  • Inventory: every car currently in stock with specs, mileage, price, and status.
  • Car Survey: tell us your favorite brand, model, color, and engine so we can match you with a car.

In the inventory, a car marked Available can be purchased now, On Hold means another buyer has a deposit on it, and Sold cars are shown for reference only.

2. Buying Process

  1. Find a car in the inventory or let us source one for you through the survey.
  2. Contact us to schedule a private showing, test drive, or virtual walkaround.
  3. Place a refundable deposit of 10% to hold the car for up to 14 days.
  4. Sign the purchase agreement and finalize payment or financing.
  5. Choose pickup at our showroom or worldwide enclosed delivery.

Test drives require a valid driver's license, proof of insurance, and a pre-approval or proof of funds for cars over $500,000.

3. Financing and Leasing

We work with lenders who specialize in exotic and collector cars.

  • Financing: terms from 36 to 144 months, with as little as 20% down.
  • Leasing: 24 to 60 month leases with a balloon payment option at the end.
  • Cash and wire transfer: accepted for all purchases.

Rates depend on credit history, down payment, and the age of the car.

4. Trade-Ins

We accept exotic, luxury, and high-performance trade-ins. To get an offer, send us:

  • Year, make, model, and VIN
  • Current mileage
  • Service records and any accident history
  • Photos of the exterior, interior, and odometer

Trade-in offers are valid for 7 days or 250 additional miles, whichever comes first.

5. Delivery and Shipping

Every car is shipped in a fully enclosed trailer to protect it from weather and road debris.

  • Local delivery: free within 100 miles of the showroom.
  • Nationwide: usually 3 to 10 days depending on distance.
  • International: shipped by sea or air freight, with import paperwork handled by our team.

6. Inspections and Warranty

Before a car is listed, it goes through a 200-point inspection covering the engine, transmission, brakes, suspension, electronics, and body. Every buyer receives the full inspection report.

Cars still under the manufacturer's warranty keep their remaining coverage. Cars outside of factory warranty come with a 6 month / 3,000 mile Crocodile Exotics powertrain warranty, and extended warranties are available.

7. Exotic Car Care

Exotic cars need more attention than everyday cars. A few tips to keep yours in top shape:

  • Let the engine warm up fully before driving hard.
  • Use a battery tender if the car will sit for more than two weeks.
  • Store it indoors under a breathable car cover.
  • Follow the manufacturer's service schedule, even if the car is driven rarely.
  • Consider paint protection film on the front end to prevent rock chips.

8. FAQ

Yes. Fill out the car survey and our team will search our network for it.
Is the deposit refundable?
